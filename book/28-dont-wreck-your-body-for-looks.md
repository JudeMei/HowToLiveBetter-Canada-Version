# 28. Don't Wreck Your Body for Looks

> The pursuit of physical perfection is heavily marketed, unregulated in parts, and potentially lethal. This chapter covers the boundaries between safe modifications and catastrophic harm.

---

### 1. Avoid extreme "crash" diets (VLCDs) without medical supervision
- Cost: Zero.
- Plain language: Very Low-Calorie Diets (under 800 calories/day) can cause rapid weight loss, but they also cause gallstones, heart arrhythmias, and massive muscle loss. They almost always result in gaining the weight back (plus more) once normal eating resumes.
- Payoff: Prevents severe metabolic and organ damage.
- Evidence: A
- Source: National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK). Very Low-Calorie Diets.
- Note: If you need to lose significant weight for health reasons (e.g., severe obesity, upcoming surgery), medical supervision is required to monitor electrolytes and heart function.

### 2. Verify the credentials of anyone injecting your face (Botox/Fillers)
- Cost: Time spent researching.
- Plain language: Injections of Botox (botulinum toxin) or dermal fillers should ONLY be performed by a regulated healthcare professional (doctor, nurse practitioner, or a registered nurse operating under medical directives). "Medical spas" operating in the back of a hair salon by someone with a weekend certificate are dangerous.
- Payoff: Prevents disfigurement, infection, and blindness.
- Evidence: B
- Source: Provincial Colleges of Physicians and Surgeons; Colleges of Nurses.
- Note: Dermal fillers injected accidentally into a blood vessel can travel to the eye and cause immediate, irreversible blindness, or cause the skin to die (necrosis). You want a professional who knows how to handle these emergencies immediately.

### 3. Do not use anabolic steroids for bodybuilding
- Cost: Saves thousands of dollars.
- Plain language: Using unprescribed testosterone or synthetic anabolic steroids to build muscle causes severe, long-term damage: severe acne, shrinking testicles, infertility, heart attacks, strokes, and liver damage.
- Payoff: Prevents irreversible endocrine system destruction and early cardiovascular death.
- Evidence: A
- Source: Health Canada. Steroids.
- Note: Once you start using steroids, your body stops producing its own testosterone. Coming off them causes severe depression, loss of libido, and muscle loss.

### 4. Do not use unregulated weight loss supplements
- Cost: Saves money.
- Plain language: The supplement industry is poorly regulated. Many "fat burners" sold online contain hidden, banned substances (like sibutramine or high doses of ephedrine) that cause fatal heart attacks and strokes.
- Payoff: Prevents sudden cardiac death.
- Evidence: A
- Source: Health Canada. Advisories and Recalls.
- Note: If a weight-loss pill actually works rapidly, it is either an illegal amphetamine or a dangerous diuretic. Safe, effective weight-loss medications (like GLP-1 agonists e.g., Ozempic, Wegovy) require a prescription and medical monitoring.

### 5. Never use indoor tanning beds
- Cost: Saves money.
- Plain language: As noted in Chapter 2, indoor tanning devices are Group 1 carcinogens (the same category as tobacco and asbestos). Using them before age 35 increases your risk of melanoma (the deadliest skin cancer) by 75%.
- Payoff: Prevents skin cancer and premature skin aging (wrinkles, leathery skin).
- Evidence: A
- Source: WHO/IARC; Canadian Cancer Society.
- Note: If you want to look tan, use a spray tan or self-tanning lotion. There is no such thing as a "safe base tan" to protect you before a vacation.

### 6. Beware of unregulated "medical tourism" for cosmetic surgery
- Cost: Prevents massive out-of-pocket costs to fix botched surgeries.
- Plain language: Flying to another country for cheap cosmetic surgery (like a "Brazilian Butt Lift" or tummy tuck) carries massive risks. If you get a life-threatening infection or the surgery is botched, you will return to Canada where the healthcare system will save your life, but will not fix the cosmetic disaster.
- Payoff: Prevents death, disfigurement, and antibiotic-resistant infections.
- Evidence: B
- Source: Canadian Society of Plastic Surgeons.
- Note: The Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL) has one of the highest mortality rates of any cosmetic procedure (due to fat embolisms in the heart and lungs). If you cannot afford the surgery in Canada with a board-certified plastic surgeon, do not get it.
